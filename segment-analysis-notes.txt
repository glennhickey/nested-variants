Ranking Strategies
------------------
Three approaches were used to identify segments enriched for polymorphic
nested variants in genic regions:

1. Raw polymorphic count (genic >30%, segment >= 1kb)
2. Balanced score: geometric mean of (polymorphic count x density per kb)
   Filters: genic >30%, >= 500bp, >= 20 polymorphic variants
3. Common polymorphisms: genic >50%, repeat <30%, AF 0.1-0.5, >= 500bp

chr19_9986_alt — PSG (Pregnancy-Specific Glycoprotein) Gene Cluster
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Source haplotype: HG01261#1#JBHDVG010000010.1:44,969,708-45,116,660
CHM13 ref coords: chr19:45,663,890-46,167,387 (503 kb on ref, 147 kb off ref)
Segment stats: 6,594 polymorphic variants, 5,292 SNPs, Ts/Tv=1.29, AF=0.33
Annotations: 79% genic, 30% repeat, 100% segdup, 0% censat

Genes in CHM13 reference region (CAT/Liftoff, 19 genes):
  PSG1, PSG2, PSG4, PSG5, PSG6, PSG7, PSG8, PSG9, PSG10P, PSG11
  CEACAMP4, CEACAMP7, CEACAMP8, CEACAMP9, CEACAMP10, CEACAMP11
  AC004784.1, AC005392.1, AC005392.2

This is the PSG gene cluster at chr19q13.2-13.3, part of the larger
carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) family. Key biology:

- 11 tandemly arrayed PSG genes, expressed almost exclusively in
  placental trophoblasts during pregnancy.
- Known copy number variation: PSG gene deletions are enriched in
  pre-eclampsia patients; low PSG levels associated with intrauterine
  growth retardation (PMID: 27753020).
- Rapidly evolving: rodent and primate PSG families expanded
  independently, likely driven by maternal-fetal conflict
  (PMID: 35007205).
- 100% segdup overlap on reference — consistent with tandem duplication
  architecture of the cluster.
- The large size discrepancy (147 kb off-ref vs 503 kb on-ref) suggests
  a substantial contraction in the HG01261 haplotype relative to CHM13,
  or complex rearrangement within the cluster.

Genes on source haplotype HG01261#1 (CAT annotation, 44,969,708-45,116,660):
  PSG2          45,093,555-45,112,384  protein_coding
  PSG11         45,037,002-45,055,828  protein_coding
  PSG11-AS1     45,006,799-45,201,181  lncRNA (antisense, extends beyond segment)
  CEACAMP7      44,978,832-44,979,716  unprocessed_pseudogene
  CEACAMP8      45,065,222-45,070,601  unprocessed_pseudogene
  ENSG00000307642  44,961,164-44,974,468  lncRNA

Full PSG cluster on HG01261#1 contig JBHDVG010000010.1 (~43.6M-45.6M):
  Protein-coding PSG genes (14 copies of 10 genes):
    PSG1 x1, PSG3 x1, PSG6 x1, PSG7 x1, PSG8 x1, PSG9 x1
    PSG2 x2, PSG4 x2, PSG5 x2, PSG11 x2
  CEACAMP pseudogenes: 13 copies
  Total cluster span: ~2 Mb

Comparison CHM13 vs HG01261#1:
  CHM13 (503 kb ref window):  10 PSG genes + 5 CEACAMP pseudogenes
  HG01261#1 (full cluster):   14 PSG gene copies (10 unique) + 13 CEACAMP

  HG01261 has MORE PSG genes than CHM13 in this window — PSG2, PSG4, PSG5,
  and PSG11 are each duplicated. The 147 kb augref segment captures an
  inserted portion of the cluster not present in the CHM13 reference at
  this position, likely part of the tandem expansion.

  The high polymorphic variant count (6,594) in this segment reflects the
  difficulty of aligning duplicated, divergent PSG paralogs within the
  pangenome graph — variants arise from paralog-specific differences
  being represented as polymorphisms.

chr4_10698_alt — FRG1-DT locus (FSHD region)
----------------------------------------------
Source: NA18508#2#JBHIJH010000004.1
CHM13 ref coords: chr4:193,196,794-193,197,379 (585 bp on ref, 31 kb off ref)
Stats: 3,445 poly, 1,604 SNPs, Ts/Tv=20.1, AF=0.23, 100% genic, 0% repeat/segdup

Gene: FRG1-DT (lncRNA divergent transcript of FRG1)
FRG1 (FSHD Region Gene 1) is a candidate gene for facioscapulohumeral
muscular dystrophy (FSHD). FRG1 protein is an actin-bundling/RNA-associated
protein critical for muscle sarcomere structure and angiogenesis.
The extremely high Ts/Tv (20.1) is notable — could indicate CpG-rich
regulatory region or strong purifying selection on transitions.
585 bp ref vs 31 kb insertion = large novel sequence not in CHM13.

chr12_2440/2441/2442 — RIMBP2 insertions (same ref locus)
-----------------------------------------------------------
All three map to CHM13 chr12:130,684,042-130,684,477 (435 bp on ref)
  chr12_2442_alt: 13 kb, 1,958 poly, Ts/Tv=0.33, AF=0.33 (NA18971#2)
  chr12_2441_alt:  9 kb, 1,418 poly, Ts/Tv=0.85, AF=0.22 (HG00438#1)
  chr12_2440_alt:  6 kb,   991 poly, Ts/Tv=1.02, AF=0.22 (HG02602#2)

Gene: RIMBP2 (RIMS Binding Protein 2) — presynaptic active zone protein
  essential for synaptic vesicle regulation and Ca2+-dependent release.
  Associated with autosomal dominant deafness (DFNA41) and centronuclear
  myopathy type 6.
Three independent haplotypes each have large insertions (6-13 kb) at the
same 435 bp ref position inside RIMBP2. Different sizes suggest multiple
independent insertion events or variable-length expansion of a repeat/
structural element within the gene. The low Ts/Tv in chr12_2442 (0.33)
suggests transversion-enriched mutations, possibly from a non-standard
mutational process.

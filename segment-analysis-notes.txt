Ranking Strategies
------------------
Three approaches were used to identify segments enriched for polymorphic
nested variants in genic regions:

1. Raw polymorphic count (genic >30%, segment >= 1kb)
2. Balanced score: geometric mean of (polymorphic count x density per kb)
   Filters: genic >30%, >= 500bp, >= 20 polymorphic variants
3. Common polymorphisms: genic >50%, repeat <30%, AF 0.1-0.5, >= 500bp

  RIMBP2:  130,433,406-130,754,746 (- strand)  last exon starts at 130,433,406
  The ref anchor (130,429,420-130,430,635) sits in the ~3 kb intergenic gap
  between PIWIL1's last exon and RIMBP2's 3'-most exon.

Structural variant hotspot:
  36 alt segments from 18 different haplotypes map to this exact 1,215 bp
  ref anchor (out of 457 total haplotypes in the pangenome = 3.9%). Segment
  sizes range from 52 bp to 3,869 bp, indicating frequent structural
  rearrangement at this locus:

    chr12_4666_alt  HG00140#2   3,869 bp  (3x expansion)

    chr12_4685_alt  HG00099#1     361 bp  (0.30x contraction)
    + 32 more fragments from 14 additional haplotypes (52-349 bp)

  The immediately adjacent ref position (130,430,640-130,430,676) has a
  further segment, chr12_2438_alt (HG00099#1, 852 bp, 9 VCF SNPs),
  making the combined hotspot ~1.26 kb.

VCF genotype pattern (7 samples: HG001-HG007):
  16 biallelic SNPs with a distinctive two-tier pattern:
  - 8 singletons (AF=0.07): only HG001 is heterozygous, all others 0/0
  - 4 common variants (AF=0.57-1.00): shared across most samples
    - 2 fixed (AF=1.00): all 7 samples homozygous alt → these positions
      differ from the NA18971 haplotype used as the segment reference
    - 2 near-fixed (AF=0.86-0.93): 6-7 samples carry alt
    - 2 intermediate (AF=0.57-0.64): 4-5 samples carry alt
  - HG007 is homozygous reference (0/0) at all non-fixed sites

  Interpretation: HG001 carries a divergent haplotype relative to the
  NA18971-derived segment, contributing 8 private variants. The fixed
  (AF=1.0) variants indicate positions where the NA18971 allele is
  itself the rare allele. HG007 appears to carry the NA18971-like
  haplotype throughout.

  Only chr12_4667_alt has called biallelic SNPs — none of the other 35
  segments at this ref anchor have any. This means the 7 call samples
  have reads mapping preferentially to the NA18971 segment sequence.

Connection to RIMBP2 insertion cluster:
  ~254 kb downstream (within the RIMBP2 gene body), three independent
  haplotypes have large insertions at another hotspot (chr12:130,684,042):
    chr12_2442_alt  NA18971#2  13,063 bp  1,958 poly  Ts/Tv=0.33
    chr12_2441_alt  HG00438#1   8,747 bp  1,418 poly  Ts/Tv=0.85
    chr12_2440_alt  HG02602#2   6,172 bp    991 poly  Ts/Tv=1.02
  Of these, only chr12_2440_alt has VCF calls (4 SNPs). Notably,
  NA18971 contributes to both clusters: the 547 bp contraction at the
  5' end and the 13 kb insertion within the gene body.

Gene biology:
  PIWIL1 (Piwi-Like RNA-Mediated Gene Silencing 1):
    - PIWI-clade Argonaute protein, essential for piRNA biogenesis
    - Silences transposable elements in the germline
    - Expressed primarily in spermatocytes; required for male fertility
    - Associated with azoospermia and male infertility (OMIM: 605571)

  RIMBP2 (RIMS Binding Protein 2):
    - Presynaptic active zone protein; scaffolds Ca2+ channels to
      synaptic vesicle release sites
    - Essential for Ca2+-triggered neurotransmitter release
    - Associated with autosomal dominant deafness DFNA41 (OMIM: 611222)
    - Associated with centronuclear myopathy type 6

  Both genes are large (PIWIL1 ~89 kb, RIMBP2 ~321 kb) and the
  structural variant hotspot sits in the regulatory/intergenic space
  between them. Structural variation in this region could affect
  regulation of either or both genes.


chr19_9986_alt — PSG (Pregnancy-Specific Glycoprotein) Gene Cluster
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Source haplotype: HG01261#1#JBHDVG010000010.1:44,969,708-45,116,660
CHM13 ref coords: chr19:45,663,890-46,167,387 (503 kb on ref, 147 kb off ref)
Segment stats: 6,594 polymorphic variants, 5,292 SNPs, Ts/Tv=1.29, AF=0.33
Annotations: 79% genic, 30% repeat, 100% segdup, 0% censat

Genes in CHM13 reference region (CAT/Liftoff, 19 genes):
  PSG1, PSG2, PSG4, PSG5, PSG6, PSG7, PSG8, PSG9, PSG10P, PSG11
  CEACAMP4, CEACAMP7, CEACAMP8, CEACAMP9, CEACAMP10, CEACAMP11
  AC004784.1, AC005392.1, AC005392.2

This is the PSG gene cluster at chr19q13.2-13.3, part of the larger
carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) family. Key biology:

- 11 tandemly arrayed PSG genes, expressed almost exclusively in
  placental trophoblasts during pregnancy.
- Known copy number variation: PSG gene deletions are enriched in
  pre-eclampsia patients; low PSG levels associated with intrauterine
  growth retardation (PMID: 27753020).
- Rapidly evolving: rodent and primate PSG families expanded
  independently, likely driven by maternal-fetal conflict
  (PMID: 35007205).
- 100% segdup overlap on reference — consistent with tandem duplication
  architecture of the cluster.
- The large size discrepancy (147 kb off-ref vs 503 kb on-ref) suggests
  a substantial contraction in the HG01261 haplotype relative to CHM13,
  or complex rearrangement within the cluster.

Genes on source haplotype HG01261#1 (CAT annotation, 44,969,708-45,116,660):
  PSG2          45,093,555-45,112,384  protein_coding
  PSG11         45,037,002-45,055,828  protein_coding
  PSG11-AS1     45,006,799-45,201,181  lncRNA (antisense, extends beyond segment)
  CEACAMP7      44,978,832-44,979,716  unprocessed_pseudogene
  CEACAMP8      45,065,222-45,070,601  unprocessed_pseudogene
  ENSG00000307642  44,961,164-44,974,468  lncRNA

Full PSG cluster on HG01261#1 contig JBHDVG010000010.1 (~43.6M-45.6M):
  Protein-coding PSG genes (14 copies of 10 genes):
    PSG1 x1, PSG3 x1, PSG6 x1, PSG7 x1, PSG8 x1, PSG9 x1
    PSG2 x2, PSG4 x2, PSG5 x2, PSG11 x2
  CEACAMP pseudogenes: 13 copies
  Total cluster span: ~2 Mb

Comparison CHM13 vs HG01261#1:
  CHM13 (503 kb ref window):  10 PSG genes + 5 CEACAMP pseudogenes
  HG01261#1 (full cluster):   14 PSG gene copies (10 unique) + 13 CEACAMP

  HG01261 has MORE PSG genes than CHM13 in this window — PSG2, PSG4, PSG5,
  and PSG11 are each duplicated. The 147 kb augref segment captures an
  inserted portion of the cluster not present in the CHM13 reference at
  this position, likely part of the tandem expansion.

  The high polymorphic variant count (6,594) in this segment reflects the
  difficulty of aligning duplicated, divergent PSG paralogs within the
  pangenome graph — variants arise from paralog-specific differences
  being represented as polymorphisms.

- https://rep.bioscientifica.com/view/journals/rep/163/2/REP-21-0390.xml (PSG evolution/function review)

chr4_10698_alt — FRG1-DT (REJECTED: tandem repeat)
---------------------------------------------------
Despite 0% repeat/segdup annotation, manual inspection of the segment
sequence reveals it is entirely composed of a ~75 bp tandem repeat
(AGCACATCACCACTGCACTAGTACACACACCATCACC...). The extreme Ts/Tv (20.1)
is an artifact of the repeat base composition. The repeat annotation
BED did not capture this element.

This segment sits within FRG1-DT (lncRNA) at chr4q35.2, 378 kb from
the telomere, proximal to the D4Z4 repeat array. The D4Z4 region
starts ~190 kb distal (chr4_8_alt: 100% censat, 100% segdup).
CHM13 ncbiRefSeq confirms: FRG1-DT (193,125-193,306 kb) → FRG1
(193,306-193,328 kb) → DUX4L9/FRG2/DBET → D4Z4 array (DUX4L
pseudogenes) → DUX4 → telomere.

Lesson: always check segment sequence for simple/tandem repeats even
when repeat annotation says 0%.
